Fertilizer Guide (Starter KB)

General principles (Kerala, humid tropics)
- Prefer split application of nitrogen to reduce loss in heavy rains.
- Use soil-test-based liming for acidic laterite soils.
- Add organic matter (compost/green manure) to improve nutrient holding.

Paddy (Rice) — generic schedule (example)
- Basal: a portion of N + full P + a portion of K.
- Tillering: N top-dress.
- Panicle initiation: N + K as needed.
- Avoid excess N in high humidity to reduce disease/pest pressure.

Banana — generic hints
- Banana is nutrient hungry; frequent split doses (N and K) are common.
- Ensure micronutrients if deficiency symptoms occur (consult local advisory).

Coconut — generic hints
- Combine organic manures with recommended NPK and micronutrients (where deficient).
- Improve drainage and mulch in summer for moisture retention.
