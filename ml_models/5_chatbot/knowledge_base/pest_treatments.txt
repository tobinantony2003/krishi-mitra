Kerala Pest & Disease Treatments (Starter KB)

Brown Plant Hopper (Rice)
- Risk: high humidity + warm temps; dense crop; excess nitrogen.
- Organic/IPM: avoid excess N, keep water level proper, encourage predators, use light traps.
- Chemical: use recommended insecticide only when ETL is crossed (consult local extension).

Stem Borer (Rice)
- Risk: high at vegetative/tillering stage in many systems.
- IPM: synchronized planting, remove stubbles, pheromone traps where available.
- Chemical: as per label and extension recommendations.

Leaf Blast (Rice)
- Risk: cool nights + very high humidity + cloudy conditions.
- Cultural: spacing, avoid late evening overhead watering, avoid excess nitrogen.
- Chemical: tricyclazole-based fungicide (follow label/extension).

Sheath Blight (Rice)
- Risk: dense canopy + high humidity.
- Cultural: reduce excess N, manage spacing, field hygiene.
- Chemical: validamycin (label/extension).

Rhinoceros Beetle (Coconut)
- Control: destroy breeding sites (rotting logs/manure pits), pheromone traps.
- Crown treatment: follow local advisory (avoid unsafe handling).

Phytophthora foot rot (Pepper)
- Control: drainage improvement, sanitation, copper-based protection in rainy months.
- Chemical: metalaxyl+mancozeb or copper fungicides (label/extension).

Note: This KB is a starter. Always align chemical use with local regulations and label instructions.
