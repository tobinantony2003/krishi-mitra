Kerala Soils (Starter KB)

Laterite
- Common in Kerala midlands and highlands.
- Often acidic; may need liming based on soil test.
- Benefits from organic matter additions to improve water/nutrient holding.

Alluvial
- Found in river basins/lowlands.
- Generally more fertile but can face flooding/waterlogging.
- Drainage management is important in monsoon.

Sandy loam
- Faster drainage, higher leaching risk.
- Needs frequent smaller fertilizer doses and organic matter to retain moisture.

Red loam
- Moderate fertility; management depends on slope and rainfall.
- Mulching helps during dry spells.

Forest soils (high ranges)
- Often high organic matter, cooler climate.
- Manage erosion; avoid excessive disturbance.

Soil health basics
- Test for pH, EC, organic carbon, NPK and micronutrients.
- Improve organic matter, reduce erosion, and maintain drainage in monsoon.
