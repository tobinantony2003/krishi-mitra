Government Schemes (India) — Starter KB

PM-KISAN
- Direct income support for eligible farmer families.
- Benefits and eligibility change over time; verify on official portal or with local agriculture office.

PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana)
- Crop insurance scheme covering notified crops/areas.
- Enrollment windows are season-based; premium subsidy applies as notified.
- For claims: timely intimation and adherence to local procedures matter.

KCC (Kisan Credit Card)
- Credit access for crop cultivation and allied activities.
- Interest subvention may apply subject to rules and timely repayment.
